STORY BACKGROUND

A MYSTERIOUS WARNING LETTER HAS ARRIVED

A masterpiece vanishes
under the moonlight.

On the eve of the Mihama Town Art Gallery's special exhibition, a red envelope arrived at the Mouri Detective Agency—a warning letter from Phantom Thief Kid.

But when Conan arrived, the curator lay unconscious, the masterpiece had vanished, and the handwriting didn't match Kid's. Someone had framed Kid. The truth is buried beneath deception and it's up to you to uncover it.
