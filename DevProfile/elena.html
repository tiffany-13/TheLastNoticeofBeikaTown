ELENA LAU WEN SHAN

Programmer | Video Editor

Hi! I'm Elena Lau Wen Shan, and I'm excited to be part of this project as the Programmer and Video Editor of the group. I come from Klang, Malaysia, a multicultural country filled with people from different races, cultures, and backgrounds. As a Malaysian Chinese, growing up in such a diverse environment has given me the opportunity to understand and communicate in three languages: English, Chinese, and Malay. Being surrounded by different cultures has also allowed me to appreciate different perspectives and ways of thinking.

I'm currently a Year Two student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Beijing Institute of Technology, China. I'm still relatively new to HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, so I see this project as a great opportunity to learn more from my teammates and improve my programming skills through hands-on experience. At the same time, I have experience in video editing, which is why I'm also responsible for creating and editing promotional videos for our game. So, if you're here after watching one of our promotional videos—yay! I guess that means my video did its job successfully.

These activities not only relax me but also train my mind to notice small details that others might overlook. Outside of academics, I'm actually a diabolo player. Also known as the Chinese yo-yo, diabolo has been one of my biggest hobbies since I was eight years old. Luckily, I was able to continue pursuing this passion at BIT, where a diabolo club was organized by my Malaysian seniors. Playing diabolo has taught me many valuable lessons, especially the importance of teamwork, communication, and cooperation when performing and practicing together.

Besides playing diabolo, I also enjoy crocheting. Although it is very different from the fast-paced movements of diabolo, crocheting has taught me to slow down and be patient. Creating something step by step with my own hands can sometimes be challenging, but it is also incredibly satisfying. Both of these hobbies have helped shape different parts of my personality and taught me valuable skills that I can carry into other areas of my life.

I'm also a big fan of games and detective-related variety shows. Because of my love for mysteries, puzzles, and solving problems, I've always wanted to create my own detective game. This project has given me the opportunity to turn that interest into something real and work together with my teammates to create an interactive experience for others. Whether you're someone who loves solving mysteries or simply enjoys playing games, I hope you have fun exploring our game and uncovering its secrets. Thank you so much for joining us on this adventure, and I hope you enjoy every moment of it!
